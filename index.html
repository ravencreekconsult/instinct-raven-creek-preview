08 — Questions and honest answers

What careful owners ask before they book.

Why pay $999 for advice I could look up?

You can find general advice anywhere. What you cannot look up is which three things are costing your shop the most, in your order of operations, with your team and your customers. The report is specific to you and ranked, so you work on what pays first. Do a quick check on your own numbers: write down the hours a week you or your office spend on quoting, scheduling and chasing payment, and what an hour of that time is worth to you. If the plan recovers even a modest share of those hours, the fee is a small number against a recurring cost.

I'm too busy for this.

That is usually the reason it is worth doing. The Assessment is two calls and a short intake, and we do the writing and analysis. You are not asked to run a project or learn a tool. [CONFIRM: call lengths]

What exactly do I get?

A written process map, the biggest time drains identified, fixes ranked by time saved, independent tool recommendations if any are warranted, and a first-steps checklist. Then a second call to go through it together. See the section above for detail.

How long does it take?

Two calls with a written report between them. [CONFIRM: turnaround, for example X business days from call one] We will give you the date when you book.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No formal prep. Answer the short intake. If you have a recent quote, an invoice and a view of your weekly schedule, keep them nearby for call one. [CONFIRM]

I already have software. Will this just tell me to buy more?

Often the opposite. We start by looking at what you already pay for and whether it is being used for what it can do. Many problems are a setup or process issue, not a missing tool. If something should be dropped or replaced, the report says so plainly.

Will you try to sell me AI or tools?

No. We do not resell software and the plan does not depend on us. AI is one option among many, recommended only when it solves a real problem in your process. If you later want help acting on the plan, you can ask. That is a separate decision, with no obligation. [CONFIRM: wording of follow-on help]

What if the plan does not help?

We cannot promise a particular result, and we will not pretend to. What we can promise is a specific, reasoned plan based on what you told us, and a second call to make sure it makes sense for your shop. [CONFIRM: refund or satisfaction policy. Nothing is promised here until you decide.]

Those are the trades we know best, so the examples lean that way. The process problems are common across service businesses: roofing, landscaping, cleaning, paving and similar.
